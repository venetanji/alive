## Geometry mechanism and uncertainty

`alive.css:23` puts open `#audio-details` at `position:absolute; bottom:0` and allows its height to follow content. Diagnostics are a four-column grid with flex labels; `alive.mjs:531-574` writes the current active-source and active-placement IDs or `-` into those outputs on render, and `alive.mjs:608` polls approximately every 80 ms after each refresh. Thus a playback transition that removes long IDs can reduce grid row heights and move the top summary downward while the bottom stays anchored. The two screenshots show precisely the expected content and 30-pixel top-position change; this is the leading causal explanation, but no per-sample DOM layout, state timestamps, or traversal durations were retained. Other factors such as focus/scroll or relayout are not excluded. The `grab_focus` occurs before sampling and the summary remains visible, so evidence does not support a missing control or focus failure.

The most recent relevant comparison, `git diff 64fcacd..a8ffe03` for the involved UI, navigation, native helper and Arrangement journey, contains only the conditional `process_sample` sharing change in `check_alive_desktop.py`; this is not in the failed path.
