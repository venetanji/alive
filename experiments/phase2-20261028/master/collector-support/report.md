## Behavior and intended integration

The helper composes the real `EpochCollector`, `PlayingIntervals`, and `JsonLines`. A driving-thread `observe(read)` accepts the existing epoch observation and transport strings together, logs a gap and invalidates continuity on unavailable/failed observations, and propagates collector and metadata failures. `action(name)` invalidates before and after the outer action and suppresses playing samples throughout nested actions. `finalize` polls through injected clock/sleep until a complete, inactive, explicitly non-retiring and quiescent observation has a nonzero, fully collected last epoch; collector failures remain fatal. `owned(cleanup, health)` polls injected health on the driving thread and after cleanup, while running cleanup even if observation/health fails. No worker or resource is started by this helper.

A future desktop driver must construct the existing bounded logs and collector before the first stream configuration, call `observe` on the UI-driving thread during sustained segments, wrap all numeric, seek, Apply, transport and editor operations in `action`, poll resource/recorder failures with `health`, and perform the existing audio stop and finalization before its original Quit. It must stop/join its own independently bounded sampler and finish/check its recorder in `cleanup`, retain logs, report, and preserve the original terminal Quit assertions. These call sites are not implemented or validated here.

## Limits

This is support preparation, not Task9 qualification, endurance, native pilot or UI integration. The helper does not spawn or join a sampler, supervise the recorder, set deadlines on a blocking UI callback, produce final manifests/reports, or perform Quit; the future driver must supply those pieces and keep them bounded. Health failures are only detected at explicit polling points and after cleanup; callers must propagate exceptions and must supply cleanup that joins any owned sampler and exposes recorder failure to health. Observation callback exceptions are treated as explicit gaps and finalization times out if valid retirement never arrives; collector/metadata failures propagate immediately. The deterministic fixtures do not validate real accessibility data, process behavior, physical audio, or UI ordering. No bounds in existing code were changed.
