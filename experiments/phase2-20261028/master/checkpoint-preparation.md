## Sustained collector implementation sketch

The short wrapper `check_alive_observability.py` is a concrete model, not a drop-in wrapper around the desktop driver: it wraps `alive.check_audio_settings`, `ui.click` and `time.sleep`, collects AT-SPI on the driving thread, samples `/proc` on a bounded worker, and runs its own finalization when the clips journey clicks Quit. Blindly stacking it over the desktop script would alter that script's Quit/cancel sequence and create competing clocks/counters. Prefer one explicit sustained collector integration with a single owner for each observation and action ordinal; keep original short test intact.

- Initialize bounded epoch/action/resource logs and collector early enough to see the first stream attempt, before the desktop `check_audio_settings()` reconfigurations. Enable/read Stream diagnostics on the UI thread with a bounded failure path; obtain initial run ID/cursor and take observations at least during the one-second sustained segment sleeps, before and after Stop/Apply/start and other stream transitions, and after each configuration change. Call `EpochCollector.accept` on each valid observation; explicitly log unavailable/error observations and break playing-interval continuity. UI-thread stalls are observation gaps, not zero measurements. Sample `/proc` separately without AT-SPI from a background thread, propagate failures and join it on all exits. Do not assume a sampling thread can safely read accessibility objects.
- The desktop loop currently advances `control_action` only in `transport()`, while numeric changes, seek, editor activity and Apply also occur between snapshots. Introduce an explicit action-boundary mechanism covering **every** workload operation that can interrupt or change playing, including numeric input/keyboard, editor open/edit/close, seek, transport and Apply; record ordinal and sample on the driving thread around boundaries. Avoid double-counting from nested wrappers. Keep one authoritative `PlayingIntervals` stream with the existing <=2.5-second gap, advancing frame and conservative frame/wall cap; any failed or absent transport observation must invalidate its previous sample. Retain existing assert that each cycle advances its native frame clock and all original actions/assertions.
- After the required playing threshold and original workload, finalize retained epochs while the process is still queryable. A safe seam is immediately after the last original assertion `pending_draft_quit_cancel_retained_edits_and_playback` and before `final-quit-entry`: stop transport and explicitly stop the audio stream through the existing audio-settings UI, then poll diagnostics with a finite deadline until retirement is complete, `active` is absent and all completed IDs are collected. This is an additional post-assertion operation; verify it does not trigger or bypass the existing dirty/pending Quit dialogs or compromise their assertions. If the UI cannot stop/retire here without changing the journey, use a separately instrumented equivalent sustained journey and leave completeness unqualified until it proves the same workload; do not infer final retirement from process disappearance. Stop/join sampler, close logs, produce manifests and finalized report, then execute and assert the original final Quit. On any finalization failure retain partial evidence, fail, and still clean owned helpers; do not label the early report as terminal success.
- A valid final receipt needs one stable run ID, contiguous completed IDs from 1, no missed retained prefix, no provisional completed record or overflow, no active/unresolved retirement, and a successful final `complete` observation; collect often enough that the 64-record ring cannot evict unseen IDs, and honor the collector's 256-attempt cap.
