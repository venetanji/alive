Audio settings

On the recorded host, use 256 frames or larger for up to two tracks / six devices, and 512 or larger for bigger sessions; prefer 1024 near the eight-track / sixteen-device bounds. Observed driver size is shown separately; settings are never changed automatically. Choose where Alive plays. These settings are saved separately from Ultra and your session.

Stop audio to change settings. Stop audio also stops transport and releases all notes. Starting audio keeps transport stopped until you press Start.
